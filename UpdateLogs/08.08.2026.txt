- Made some small improvements to texts.csv (very small improvement but nice in the long run)

- 2 minutes Normal Battle duration
- 30 seconds Final Stretch duration

2. Additions

-> New rewards for Squad League (or Ranked if you wanna call it like that) including:
- Much more coins and Elixir
- You can now get Unlock Tokens and Emote Packs
- Common, Rare, Epic and Diamond chests are also there

3. Balance Changes

-> Goblin
Coins when spawned (8 -> 5)

-> Battle Healer
HP (900 -> 1300)
DMG (120 -> 130)
Why: Battle Healer has some decent healing value but can get some buffs to make her fit even better in her Healer class.

-> Max
HP (650 -> 1300)
DMG (250 -> 200)
Why: As a Speedster, Max is really good at her job but could get some adjustments. Speedster Squaddies shouldn't be very opressive and this will allow her to be more strategic.

-> Shelly
HP (700 -> 1400)
DMG (190 -> 200)
Why: Shelly is decent as an All-Rounder so these changes should make her a great choice in battles.

-> Bo
HP (400 -> 1200)
DMG (150 -> 200)
Attack Speed +25% faster
Why: Bo was always underperfoming out of all Squaddies and these buffs should make him much more stable and playable.

-> Nita
HP (700 -> 1500)
DMG (100 -> 110)
Why: Nita doesn't have enough HP to be a Defender so these buffs should justify her role.

-> Bea
HP (500 -> 1000)
DPS (200 -> 210)
Why: Attackers are usually pretty fragile in terms of HP but have good damage output. These buffs should make Bea even better.

Aaand with that all Desert World characters are rebalanced. Stay tuned for the rest of the Squaddies getting rebalanced soon ;)
